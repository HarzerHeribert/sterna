Why Sterna ↓

THAT’S WHY IT’S
CALLED STERNA.

STERNA Trades Endless Round-trips for Nested Actions. It is named for Sterna paradisaea, the Arctic tern, which flies further each year than any other animal.

How a turn works

ONE PROGRAM.

Most agents go back to the model after every tool call. Sterna’s model writes the whole step at once, as one TypeScript program, and every call in it runs before the model is asked again.

Results stay live

What a tool returns stays in the program under a name. The model reads a short preview and opens the rest when it needs it.

Nothing waits in between

Reads and searches run side by side, and the program branches on real results without another round trip.

Long sessions keep their results

When the context fills up, the conversation is compacted. The results stay where they are.

Around the turn

HELPERS.

Narrow questions go to a cheaper model from inside the program, so they never cost a turn of the main one.

Find, reduce, check

A helper finds where something lives, cuts a log down to its failures, or tests a claim against the evidence.

Subagents beside the turn

A separate goal goes to a subagent, which works alongside and reports back when it is done.

Done the way your repo says

Sterna reads your AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md and runs the checks they ask for before it calls a change finished.

YOUR SUBSCRIPTION.
YOUR KEYS.

Sign in with the ChatGPT or Claude plan you already have, or bring an API key. Sterna starts its own gateway beside itself, and that is where they stay.

Keys never reach the model

Keys and logins stay in the gateway: never in the conversation, the transcript or a log.

Switch models mid-task

Move between ChatGPT, Claude and other providers without starting over.

You decide how often it asks

From every call to never, inside the operating system’s own sandbox.

$curl -fsSL https://harzerheribert.github.io/sterna/install.sh | sh
01

Install

One line, on macOS, Linux or Windows. It checks the release against its checksums before it installs anything.

02

Sign in

Start sterna in a project and type /login: a ChatGPT or Claude subscription, or an API key.

03

Say what you need

“Fix the failing test.” Sterna reads, edits, checks, and tells you what it did.
